On the Genealogy of Morals & Ecce Homo

Written by: Friedrich Nietzsche

Synopsis

On the Genealogy of Morals is a philosophical work by Friedrich Nietzsche, published in 1887. In this book, Nietzsche explores the origins of moral values and critiques traditional morality, particularly the concepts of good and evil blaming Christianity for the most part for the influence these concepts have on society.

Big Ideas

  • Genealogy of Morality
  • Master-Slave Morality
  • Will to Power
  • Transvaluation of Values
