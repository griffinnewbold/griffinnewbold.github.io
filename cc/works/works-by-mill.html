On Liberty, Utilitarianism & Other Essays

Written by: John Stuart Mill

Synopsis

On Liberty, Utilitarianism & Other Essays is a collection of philosophical essays by John Stuart Mill, published in 1859. The collection includes Mill's works on individual liberty and utilitarianism, in which he makes the case for individual liberties along with a utilitarian way of thinking.

Big Ideas

  • Individual Liberty
  • Utilitarianism
  • Harm Principle
  • Tyranny of the Majority
