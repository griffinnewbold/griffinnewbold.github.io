The Souls of Black Folk

Written by: W. E. B. Du Bois

Other Works Read by Du Bois: The Culture of White Folk

Synopsis

The Souls of Black Folk is a work by W. E. B. Du Bois, published in 1903. In this book, Du Bois presents a collection of essays that explore the experiences of African Americans in the United States following the abolition of slavery.

Big Ideas

  • Double Consciousness
  • Racial Discrimination and Injustice
  • Educational Empowerment
  • Pan-Africanism
