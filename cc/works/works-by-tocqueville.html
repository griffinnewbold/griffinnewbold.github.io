Democracy in America

Written by: Alexis de Tocqueville

Other Works Read by Tocqueville: Essay on Algeria

Synopsis

Democracy in America, written by Alexis de Tocqueville and published in two volumes in 1835 and 1840, is a major work involvingthe American political system and society. Tocqueville explores the advantages and disadvantages of democracy in the United States, examining all of its implications.

Big Ideas

  • Tyranny of the Majority
  • Individualism
  • Role of Civil Society
