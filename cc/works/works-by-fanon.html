The Wretched of the Earth

Written by: Frantz Fanon

Synopsis

The Wretched of the Earth is a work by Frantz Fanon, published in 1961. In this book, Fanon explores the psychology of colonization and the effects of colonialism on colonized peoples. He discusses the dynamics of colonial oppression and the struggle for liberation.

Big Ideas

  • Colonial Oppression and Violence
  • Psychological Effects of Colonization
  • Decolonization and Liberation
  • Violence and Revolution
  • Building a New Humanity
