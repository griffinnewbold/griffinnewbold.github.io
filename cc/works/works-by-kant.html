Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals

Written by: Immanuel Kant

Other Works Read by Kant: "What is Enlightenment?"

Synopsis

Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals, by Immanuel Kant, discusses the fundamental principles of moral philosophy. Published in 1785, it lays the groundwork for Kantian ethics. Kant explores the concept of duty and the categorical imperative.

Big Ideas

  • Duty
  • Categorical Imperative
  • Autonomy of the Moral Agent
