Topic Debates


Welcome to the portal for the topic debates. Here you can read authors in dialogues on a particular subject, if the subject is outside the realm of either author their views are extrapolated from my interpretation of their works. Each piece is focused on elements relevant to the discussions from the semester. The format that these pieces are delivered in is mini essays, so these are less formal than the traditional essay like was submitted for the midterm paper.



Topic Debate #1:


Smith v. Kant

A look back towards an original midterm prompt but slightly revised, we examine Smithian and Kantian morality and discuss a potential alternative to their approaches.

Topic Debate #2:


Michel Foucault v. The City of New York

Michel Foucault's perspective on discipline and how it can be seen as a form of restorative justice conflicts with New York's version of restorative justice. Take a deep dive into Foucault's vision along with New York's execution and see where these pathes diverge and ultimately where New York went wrong.
